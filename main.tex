By the end of the internship I had built a solid toolkit in data science and system development. I practiced the full data workflow—collection, cleaning, analysis, modeling, and iteration—and trained churn-prediction and customer-segmentation models using Logistic Regression, XGBoost, LightGBM, Random Forest, CatBoost, K-Means, DBSCAN, and Gaussian Mixture Models. I learned how to evaluate models, visualize findings, and turn insights into personalized marketing recommendations. On the engineering side, I designed data pipelines, built APIs with FastAPI, and deployed an operational dashboard in Streamlit. The internship also sharpened my communication skills: preparing slides, presenting to stakeholders, running demos, and facilitating a seminar with interactive quizzes.

On a personal note, I found the workplace professional, welcoming, and genuinely supportive of learning and experimentation. Teammates and mentors offered timely guidance and constructive feedback that accelerated my growth. The program struck a good balance between theory and real-world application, giving me hands-on experience with the end-to-end workflow of AI/Data projects. It was a valuable experience that strengthened my motivation to pursue this field further.
