\subsection{Introduction to GOSU Corporation}

\subsubsection{General Information}

GOSU Online Corporation, founded in 2012, operates in information technology and digital content, with a particular focus on producing, developing, and publishing online games. As one of the leading game publishers in Vietnam, GOSU currently has three representative offices in Hanoi, Hue, and Ho Chi Minh City. The company has successfully operated many well–known titles such as \emph{Cửu Âm Chân Kinh}, \emph{Con Đường Tơ Lụa Origin Mobile}, and \emph{Ngạo Kiếm Vô Song Origin}. GOSU received the SME100 Award recognizing outstanding SMEs in Asia and ranked among the Top 6 largest game publishers in Vietnam at GameVerse 2024. Beyond publishing, GOSU also focuses on “Make in Vietnam” game production and is gradually expanding Vietnamese game products to international markets (Website: \url{https://gosucorp.vn/vi}).

\subsection{Internship Content}

\subsubsection{Objectives}

The internship topic is \emph{“Applying AI to Churn Prediction and Player Clustering for Personalized Game Marketing.”} The goal is to build AI models that analyze player behavior to early predict churn and cluster players by spending patterns, preferences, and playtime. This supports the company in deploying retention and personalized marketing strategies such as gifting, suggesting engaging quests, and optimizing promotional content, top–up packages, and item rewards.

\subsubsubsection*{Task Description}

\subsubsubsection*{A. Training churn prediction models}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Training data preparation}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Used the preprocessed and standardized dataset from Week 3.

        \item Split data by spending behavior:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Payers:} players with at least one top–up transaction.

            \item \textbf{Non–Payers:} players who have never spent.
        \end{itemize}

        \item \textbf{Rationale for the split:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item The two groups often show different behavior patterns; training separate models improves prediction accuracy.

            \item Avoids spending–related features “contaminating” predictions for non–spenders.
        \end{itemize}

        \item \textbf{For each group:}
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item \textbf{Select appropriate features:}
            \begin{itemize}
                \item \textbf{Payers:} login frequency, days since last login, total top–up amount, days since last top–up, item upgrade counts, activity/engagement indices, …

                \item \textbf{Non–Payers:} login frequency, days since last login, item upgrade counts, activity/engagement indices, …
            \end{itemize}

            \item Split into Train/Test with an 80:20 ratio.
        \end{enumerate}
    \end{itemize}
     
    \item \textbf{Training multiple models}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Logistic Regression – interpretable baseline.

        \item Random Forest (RF) – robust for many features, little preprocessing.

        \item XGBoost – strong for tabular data with high performance.

        \item LightGBM – fast training, good for large data.

        \item CatBoost – handles categorical features well and reduces overfitting.

        \item \textbf{Procedure:}
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item For each model, apply \texttt{RandomizedSearchCV} to find the best hyperparameters on the training set.

            \item Evaluate each tuned model on the test set.

            \item Save tuned models for inclusion in a Voting Classifier.
        \end{enumerate}
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Ensembling (Voting Classifier)}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Combined predictions from multiple models.

        \item \textbf{Hard Voting:} choose the label predicted by the majority.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Model evaluation}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Metrics: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1–score, ROC–AUC.

        \item Compared single models with the Voting model:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Voting improved stability and reduced the risk of misclassification on edge cases.
        \end{itemize}

        \item Analyzed feature importance from tree–based models (RF, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) to identify the most influential churn drivers.
    \end{itemize}

\end{itemize}
